# Submission email to the PPA Right-to-Know Officer

How to submit, as the Parking Authority's transparency page
(<https://philapark.org/transparency/>) stated on 2026-10-15:

- **Address:** `openrecordsofficer@philapark.org`. Right to Know Officer: Alanna D. Buchanan, Esq.,
  701 Market Street, Suite 5400, Philadelphia, PA 19106.
- **The request must be written and must not be anonymous.** No reason for the request is
  required (Sections 702–703).
- **The form:** the page links PPA's own form, `2025-RTK-Request-Form-1.pdf`, from its
  "Right To Know Request Form" link. Use that form rather than the generic Office of Open Records
  form named in `README.md`. Fill it in, put "See Attachment A" in the records-requested box, and
  attach `rtkl-request-ppa.md` as Attachment A (PDF).
- **Not checked here:** the form's own fields, including any residency affirmation box, and the
  policy PDF's wording on accepted formats. Both PDFs open as downloads, so neither was read.
  Check every box on the form before sending.

**To:** openrecordsofficer@philapark.org
**Subject:** Right-to-Know Law request: on-street parking transaction, permit and citation records
**Attachments:** completed PPA Right-to-Know request form (PDF); Attachment A, records requested (PDF)

Dear Ms. Buchanan,

Please accept the attached request under the Pennsylvania Right-to-Know Law. The completed
request form is attached, and Attachment A sets out the records requested in full; it is part of
the request.

In brief, for January 1, 2023 through the most recent month available, the request seeks:

1. Session-level transaction records for on-street meters and meterUP / Parkmobile.
2. Residential Permit Parking issuance records.
3. On-street parking citation records.
4. Reference files: the metered-location inventory with rates and hours of operation, RPP zone
   boundaries, and data dictionaries for the above.

I request the records electronically, by email or a shared link, in whatever format costs the
Authority least to produce. Please notify me before processing if fees will exceed $50. Where a
field is exempt, such as a license plate, I ask that it be redacted or hashed and the rest of the
record released, under Section 706. If any part of the request would be burdensome, I would
welcome a conversation about narrowing its scope before a denial.

Please confirm receipt and the tracking number assigned to this request.
