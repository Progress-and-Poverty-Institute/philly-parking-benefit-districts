# Submission email to the PPA Right-to-Know Officer

How to submit, per the Authority's transparency page (<https://philapark.org/transparency/>) and
its Right to Know Law Policy, both read on 2026-10-15:

- **Form.** The Authority considers only requests made on its own Request Form (policy
  section 4(b)). The page links the 2025 version from "Right To Know Request Form". Fill in one
  form per request: one for Attachment A, one for Attachment B. Field-by-field notes are at the
  top of `rtkl-request-ppa.md`.
- **Address.** Email `OpenRecordsOfficer@philapark.org`. Right to Know Officer: Alanna D.
  Buchanan, Esq., 701 Market Street, Suite 5400, Philadelphia, PA 19106. Fax (215-683-9619),
  mail and hand delivery are also accepted.
- **Timing.** Requests received after 4:30 p.m., or on a day the office is closed, count as
  received the next business day. The five-business-day response period starts the business day
  after receipt, and the Authority may extend it once by up to 30 calendar days.
- **Fees.** Delivery by email and redaction carry no fee. "Specialized documents", which may
  include bulk exports, are charged at up to actual cost and must be paid before the records are
  released.
- **Related request.** Do not resend anything covered by No. 26-0145, the parking request the
  road-pricing project filed on 2026-10-06. Its response is due 13 November 2026, and both
  attachments exclude it by name.

Attach four PDFs: two completed forms, Attachment A and Attachment B (both in
`rtkl-request-ppa.md`).
